# Metadata curation log

Every manual change to a downloaded sample sheet goes here, with the date,
the source of the correction, and the reason. Public sample metadata is
frequently incomplete or inconsistently labelled, and an uncorrected
sample sheet is the most common silent error in reanalysis of public data.

Format:

| date | accession | sample | field | from | to | source | reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-01-01 | PRJNA000000 | SRR0000001 | arm | (blank) | treated | Table S2 of the paper | ENA sample attribute missing |
